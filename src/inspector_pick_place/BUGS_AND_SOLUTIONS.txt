[BUG 04] README listed inspector_perception + overhead camera but neither was in the repo.
  Fix: added the package, a camera sensor in the world, and a ros_gz_bridge for the image topics.
[BUG 05] Table objects unreachable with the original joint limits.
  Cause: tool is collinear with the forearm, so reaching x=0.4, z=0.43 needs joint_3 ~1.7 rad.
  Fix: joint_3 limit widened to +/-2.4 rad.
[BUG 06] A 4-DOF arm cannot do Cartesian paths in MoveIt (tool orientation not controllable).
  Fix: straight-line approach/lift/retreat are IK-interpolated and sent to arm_controller directly.
[NOTE 07] Red/blue cubes are yawed +/-0.2915 rad so the parallel jaws meet flat faces.
